# Programme review, October

These notes are a made-up example for trying Nebula.

## Decisions

- Teacher training moves from weekdays to Saturdays from 1 November, because weekday attendance was low.
- Each district sends its centre report by the fifth of every month.
- The target for Guntur is 450 active centres by March.

## Discussion

Attendance at weekday training sessions averaged 58% in September. Centre staff said Saturdays clash
with fewer household duties. Krishna district will pilot short refresher sessions in December.
